## 🌟 Top Civic Tech & Open Government Data Analytics Ecosystem

> **A curated catalog of enterprise SaaS/hosted platforms and open-source GitHub frameworks powering open data portals, participatory democracy, public budgeting, civic GIS, FOIA compliance, and citizen engagement analytics.**

*Last updated: November 2026*

This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** in **Civic Tech & Open Government Data Analytics**. These tools publish, catalog, visualize, audit, and analyze public datasets, municipal budgets, legislative proceedings, Freedom of Information (FOIA) requests, and citizen engagement workflows across municipal portals, regional bodies, national open-data hubs, and civic research platforms.

Whether you are a municipal IT leader, civic technologist, policy researcher, data journalist, urban planner, or open-government advocate, this list helps you evaluate software solutions and deploy production-grade civic infrastructure.

## 📊 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global dedicated **Civic Engagement Platforms & Civic Tech software market** is estimated at **$3.0 billion to $3.5 billion** (growing at an 11.4% CAGR), nested within the broader **$850+ billion global GovTech ecosystem**. The market exhibits a **moderately fragmented structure**: foundational public-sector ERP, land licensing, and enterprise records are concentrated among legacy GovTech conglomerates (e.g., Tyler Technologies, Granicus, Cox/OpenGov), while the public data analytics, participatory budgeting, AI sentiment intelligence, and community engagement segments remain highly fragmented across innovative high-growth startups and open-source consortia.

## 🛠️ Frameworks for Custom Architectures

When constructing custom civic digital systems, recommended open-source stack pairings include:

1. **National / Municipal Open Data Portals**: Combine **[CKAN](https://github.com/ckan/ckan)** or **[uData](https://github.com/opendatateam/udata)** as the core DCAT metadata engine with **[Portal.js](https://github.com/datopian/portaljs)** for modern Next.js user-facing interfaces.
2. **Citizen Consultations & Participatory Budgets**: Deploy **[Decidim](https://github.com/decidim/decidim)** or **[Consul Democracy](https://github.com/consuldemocracy/consuldemocracy)** paired with **[Pol.is](https://github.com/compdemocracy/polis)** for real-time consensus mapping.
3. **Open GIS & Spatial Infrastructure**: Integrate **[QGIS](https://github.com/qgis/QGIS)** for spatial vector authoring and **[TerriaJS](https://github.com/TerriaJS/terriajs)** for browser-based 3D digital-twin maps.
4. **Data Journalism & Records Auditing**: Use **[Datasette](https://github.com/simonw/datasette)** alongside **[Froide](https://github.com/okfde/froide)** or **[Alaveteli](https://github.com/mysociety/alaveteli)** to ingest, redact, and publicly search Freedom of Information releases.

## Public Data APIs (Apify)

- [City Council Meetings Scraper (Legistar)](https://apify.com/publicrecords/legistar-meetings-scraper) - City council meetings, agenda items and documents for any Legistar city (Apify Actor, API + MCP).
- [USAspending Awards Scraper](https://apify.com/publicrecords/usaspending-awards-scraper) - US federal contract and grant awards by agency, recipient, NAICS and period (Apify Actor, API + MCP).
- [Grants.gov Scraper](https://apify.com/publicrecords/grants-gov-scraper) - Open US federal grants: agency, deadline, eligibility and source link (Apify Actor, API + MCP).
- [Federal Contracts Feed (SAM.gov + Grants.gov)](https://apify.com/publicrecords/govcon-opportunity-feed) - New US federal contract and grant notices from SAM.gov and Grants.gov in one feed (Apify Actor, API + MCP).
- [EU TED Tenders Scraper](https://apify.com/publicrecords/eu-ted-notices-scraper) - EU procurement notices from TED: buyer, CPV, value, deadline, link (Apify Actor, API + MCP).
- [UK Tenders Scraper](https://apify.com/publicrecords/uk-find-a-tender-scraper) - UK government tenders from Find a Tender and Contracts Finder: buyer, value, deadline (Apify Actor, API + MCP).
